Writing tips
  • Mention a day ("yesterday", "on Monday", "2025-06-01") and it becomes the log date.
  • Shorthand works: 5x5, 3x10, 10,8,6, bodyweight.
  • Mix it all in one message: lifts go to your exercises, a walk, run or swim goes to cardio, and "weighed in at 130" records your body weight.
  • Units are kept as you write them: 185 lbs or 80 kg.
